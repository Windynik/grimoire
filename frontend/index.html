      How do I export my collection?
      • ManaBox: open Collection, tap ⋯, choose Export, then CSV.
      • Moxfield, Archidekt or Deckbox: open your collection and choose Export as CSV.
      • Dragon Shield, TCGplayer, TopDecked or MTGGoldfish: use the CSV export of the collection.
      • A card list: save it as a .txt file, one card on each line, such as 4 Lightning Bolt.

      Send the file to this computer (AirDrop works), then drop it on the binder.
