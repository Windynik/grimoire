      Set up Grimoire

      Grimoire stores your cards on this Mac. You can search them and build decks from them. The setup takes about a minute.

      1. 1

        Download card data

        Grimoire downloads the Scryfall card data once (about 100 MB). After that, Grimoire works offline.

      2. 2

        Import your collection

        Import a collection export (CSV) or a card list (.txt), or try the sample collection.

      3. 3

        Open your collection

      How do I export my collection?
      • ManaBox: open Collection, tap ⋯, choose Export, then CSV.
      • Moxfield, Archidekt or Deckbox: open your collection and choose Export as CSV.
      • Dragon Shield, TCGplayer, TopDecked or MTGGoldfish: use the CSV export of the collection.
      • A card list: save it as a .txt file, one card on each line, such as 4 Lightning Bolt.

      Send the file to this computer (AirDrop works), then drop it in the box above.
